- Evolution cards move 4 → 2 → 1 columns.
- Home cards move 4 → 2 → 1 columns.
- Work artwork moves 3 → 2 → 1 columns with no forced horizontal card overflow.
- Services move to stacked cards with readable presentation-scale typography.
- About model and stat blocks stack cleanly.
- Business/Career cards and forms stack cleanly.
- Inquiry modal becomes true 100dvh full-screen on mobile.
- Mobile input text is 16px to prevent iOS zoom.
- Footer moves 3 → 2 → 1 columns with safe email/address wrapping.
- Touch devices expose episode play actions without requiring hover.
